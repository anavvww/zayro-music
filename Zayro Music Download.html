About this app

Zayro Music is a free Android music app developed by ANAV STUDIO. It is built around one job — playing your music well. Open the app, find a song, and press play. Playback continues in the background, so your music keeps playing while you use other apps or turn the screen off.

Your listening stays organised with favorites and your own playlists, and songs you download are available offline. Lyrics are shown in the fullscreen player, an equalizer lets you shape the sound, and a sleep timer can stop playback when you drift off. The app follows your style with both light and dark themes — and it contains no advertisements.

  • Play Songs

    Fast, smooth playback in a clean fullscreen player, with background play.

  • Offline Downloads

    Download songs to your device and listen without an internet connection.

  • Favorites

    Mark the songs you love and find them again instantly.

  • Playlists

    Create and manage your own playlists for every mood and moment.

  • Shuffle Play

    Shuffle your songs and let the app surprise you with what plays next.

  • Lyrics

    Read and sing along with lyrics while your song is playing.

  • Ringtone Maker

    Turn a favourite part of a song into your phone ringtone.

  • Sleep Timer

    Set a timer and playback stops on its own when time is up.

  • Equalizer

    Adjust the sound to your taste with the built-in equalizer.

  • Light & Dark Themes

    Choose a light or dark look, or follow your system theme.

What's new

Version 2.0.2

Version 2.0.2 makes Zayro Music a simpler, fully standalone app. Systems the app does not need right now were removed, so it runs as one normal, single app. No new features were added in this version — the simplification itself is the change.

  • Fully standalone: the app now works entirely on its own, as a single normal app.
  • Home-screen widgets removed for now — they may return in a future version.
  • In-app self-updates removed for now — new versions are shared as a fresh download.
  • Remote owner controls removed for now — nothing controls the app from outside.
  • Poster style removed — the player now uses the standard fullscreen style.
  • Battery-optimization permission removed — background play still runs through the media service.
  • Everything else is unchanged — playback, downloads, favorites, playlists, lyrics, themes and all other features work exactly as before.
